How to read this dashboard
  • Week-to-week swings are mostly noise. One especially successful piece or a slow news cycle moves these numbers more than anything we do, so trust the long-run trend lines over the latest data point.
  • Engagement matters more than raw traffic. A smaller audience that reads to the end, comes back and subscribes is worth far more than a spike of one-time visitors who never return.
  • Think in terms of the funnel. Visitors count when they convert to subscribers, subscribers count when they stay engaged and engaged readers are the ones who eventually give, share or act — so watch where people drop off, not just how many arrive.
  • Who reads matters more than how many. Vital City exists to move policy, so a few hundred mayors, commissioners, council staffers, funders and reporters reading closely can count for more than tens of thousands of casual visitors.

A year of our own X posts — what actually worked · X analytics exports (content + daily), Aug. 2025-Aug. 2026 · 418 posts, 365 tracked days · not live, refreshed by hand

Maps convert; virality doesn't. Four posts sharing interactive maps produced 14,711 link clicks and 220 new followers — 65% of the year's clicks and 60% of its new followers, at 45 clicks per 1,000 impressions. The year's viral hit — a Mamdani rent-freeze video clip that reached 10.1 million impressions, 82% of the year's total — delivered 809 clicks and 17 followers: reach without conversion, at 0.08 clicks per 1,000.

The feed is a lottery. The median post reached 420 people; the top 20 of 418 posts accounted for 95% of all impressions. What breaks out: data that corrects a common belief (the asking-rent-vs-actual-rent thread, 278,000 impressions), officials in their own words (the Lieber and Mamdani quote posts) and anything interactive.

By theme, clicks per 1,000 impressions with the viral clip set aside: maps 45 · transit 9 · crime 6 · housing 4 · mayoral politics 2. Mayoral content earns the most impressions and converts the worst; the tools convert 20 times better than commentary. Year totals: 12.4M impressions, 22,553 link clicks, 364 follows attributed to specific posts.

The account, day by day (from the separate daily export): the follower ledger ran +1,999 gross, −182 unfollows, net +1,817 for the year — and the two days after the map post account for 409 of those follows, a fifth of the year's gross in 48 hours. Posting pays double: the median posting day reaches 8,300 impressions, the median quiet day 3,900. Attention tracks the news cycle — election week and the map spike drove the peaks, and the daily baseline otherwise runs 5,000-10,000 impressions.

One reconciliation caveat: X's two exports disagree. The daily feed only starts Aug. 27, 2025 and shows zero for the day of the 10.1M-impression post; per-post lifetime impressions land on the post, not the day. So the daily-file year (4.6M impressions) and the post-file year (12.4M) measure different things and cannot be added or compared.

Media mentions edit whitelist ↗

Third-party publications citing Vital City. Searches Google News for the exact phrase, the @ handle, and any vitalcitynyc.org URL share — inside a whitelist of 32 outlets (Gothamist, NYT, NY1, WNYC, Streetsblog, THE CITY, Politico, NY Daily News, NY Mag, City Journal, City & State, City Limits, Gotham Gazette, amNewYork, ABC7, SILive, W42ST, Real Deal, plus key Substacks, plus national: New Yorker, WaPo, Bloomberg, Guardian, Newsweek, Semafor, Fast Company, Law.com, Nieman Lab, LinkedIn, X). Limits to know: WNYC audio segments aren't indexed by Google News (Gothamist co-publishes some WNYC content and catches those); paywalled stories that don't expose the phrase to crawlers will miss; podcast mentions miss entirely (a podcast audit found appearances Google News never indexes). Government, academic and staff-appearance mentions are tracked separately below, because an outlet whitelist can’t see them. To add an outlet, see the whitelist editing guide.

    Cited in government & policy the mentions that move decisions

    Official statements, testimony and reports that cite Vital City, plus republications by policy institutions (Citizens Budget Commission, Niskanen, Center for an Urban Future, Brennan Center and others). A Public Advocate report citing our research is worth more than a passing news mention — this is the channel a press-only whitelist misses entirely.

        Google News does not index audio, so no whitelist — however long — will ever catch a podcast or radio appearance. These are logged by hand from the mentions audit and are the only list here that doesn’t refresh itself. Add one by editing mentions_ledger.json.
