People Vital City already knows

The curated press list, and anyone whose story matched a logged Vital City mention. Having written for Vital City does not put anyone here. These are the names set in bold everywhere else on the page. Some have no harvested byline — they are on the list, not in a feed — so this is the only place they appear.

By outlet

Every outlet in the map. People is how many of its journalists the harvest could name; a dash means no roster is reachable, which is a fact about the outlet's crawler policy and not about its importance — pitch the tips line. Bylined stories counts stories that carried an author; items harvested counts everything, including the headline-only feeds that come through Google News.
