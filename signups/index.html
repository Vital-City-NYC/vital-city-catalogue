Signups (Ghost) Unsubscribes (Mailchimp) +12 Net for the day Dates under the bars are Thursdays, when the newsletter goes out.

Where the numbers come from. The growth dashboard's nightly file (growth_pull.py), the same daily series its signup charts use. Signups are dated by the day someone joined through the Ghost form. Unsubscribes are dated by the day they happened in Mailchimp, where people unsubscribe from the email footer; Ghost only learns of them in the Thursday sync, so its own dates run late. Signups count everyone who joined, including people who later left.

Scale. Every month since April 2026, when the site moved to Ghost, is drawn on one scale, so bar heights compare directly. The scale is set by ordinary days; the rare day that runs past it (the July 2, 2026 event-list import added 134) is drawn cut off, with its number given under the chart. Earlier months are each drawn on their own scale, because the 2025 bot signups would flatten everything else; the page says so on those months.

The file is rebuilt overnight, so today is missing and the last day can be short. The growth dashboard has the monthly view and the key indicators.
