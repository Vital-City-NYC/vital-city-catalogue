How it works 🌙 Dark mode Lock

Media mentions

Every publication, government document and program that has cited Vital City, newest first. Each press mention is tagged by how it uses our work. This is the same list the growth dashboard and the press page show.

In the press

Third-party outlets citing Vital City, found by Google News searches for the exact phrase, the @vitalcitynyc handle and any vitalcitynyc.org link, inside a whitelist of outlets. Pages the check found only naming us in a sidebar or related-stories box are left out.

    Government and policy

    Official statements, testimony and reports that cite Vital City, plus republications by policy institutions. Each page was fetched and checked, because "vital city" is ordinary English on government sites.

      Podcasts, radio and video

      Logged by hand, because Google News does not index audio.

        Where this comes from. The press tracker runs every night (growth_pull.py) and writes one feed. This page, the growth dashboard and the press page all read that feed and apply the same rules, kept in one file (mentions.js), so the three lists always match. Outlets on the whitelist can be changed; the growth dashboard's guide explains how.

        What it leaves out. Paywalled stories that hide the phrase from search engines, and podcast or radio appearances nobody has logged. This is not the citation count. The number used in the key indicators and on the home page is the stricter impact ledger in Prospects, which drops roundups, passing mentions and duplicates.
