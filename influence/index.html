How each Vital City hit was checked

"Vital City" is also ordinary English, so every press and government hit was opened and read. Unreadable pages (paywalls, bot walls) are counted at the rate confirmed on readable ones.

Newsletter subscribers with a New York government work address, month by month. This is Vital City's own list, so it has no peer comparison and is not part of the index. It is reach into government, one step removed from influence.

The shaded band on the index chart is the separate question of counting noise: every count redrawn 3,000 times as random variation around what was observed.

Method

Everything needed to rebuild these numbers. The code is influence_pull.py (collection) and influence_build.py (scoring) in the toolkit repository; influence/methodology.md carries the same text.
The concept, the choice of peers and measures, and the editorial judgments here are the author's. The collection code, the scoring code and this page were written mostly by Anthropic's Claude (Claude Code) under close direction. Quality checks performed on this project: every Vital City press and government hit was opened and read by script and classified with a capitalized-name test; the court count was deduplicated by case after one filing's attachments were found to repeat nine times; Scholar counts are copied from Scholar's own result line and kept with the text it printed; Common Crawl ranks were read from Common Crawl's published files for every release; and the index was recomputed under nine alternative choices of weights, measures and peers. The risks of using AI remain, and we would be grateful to hear of any red flags you notice.
